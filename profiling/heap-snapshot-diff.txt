Conclusion: nothing owned by a match survives leaving it. About 12 KB of generic objects appear over
three cycles (runtime/library bookkeeping), which does not grow with match length or entity count.

Reading: the per-cycle JSHeapUsedSize growth is dominated by compiled code (V8 optimising the
functions that run hot during combat), not by data retained from previous matches. See
memory-12-cycles.log for the trend over twelve cycles on the production build.
